PROPOSED COMMUNICATIONS STACK

Microsoft 365 with managed identity

For a small Las Vegas-based consultancy with geographically dispersed contributors, PCP should standardize business communications on a managed cloud suite instead of maintaining ad hoc email accounts, local files and loosely coordinated project records. The specific subscription tier and price must be validated during procurement.

Microsoft Teams

Use defined project channels for discussions, meetings, issue triage, deadlines and consultant coordination. Apply membership reviews and separate client-facing spaces from internal administration.

SharePoint and OneDrive

Keep controlled project documents and deliverables in SharePoint team sites; use OneDrive for individual working files, not as the system of record for shared client engagements. Set versioning, retention and sharing restrictions.

Exchange Online / Outlook

Consolidate company email and calendars under managed accounts. Configure domain authentication, anti-phishing defenses, shared mailboxes where needed, and timely account offboarding.

Entra ID and endpoint management

Centralize access and account lifecycle. Require MFA and conditional policies, use managed/compliant devices for sensitive business workflows, and evaluate Microsoft Intune according to the selected licensing and device fleet.

Phased adoption recommendation

  1. Discover and inventory: Identify users, contractor relationships, communication systems, shared drives, data classifications, domains and existing licensing.
  2. Establish security baseline: Configure the tenant, user identities, MFA, device standards, approved external collaboration, and recovery administrators. Separately provision administrative identities and privileged-access workstations (PAWs); establish Entra PIM eligibility, activation rules, and scoped permissions, subject to licensing.
  3. Pilot with office staff and a consultant cohort: Test remote access, meetings, file permissions, migration, mobile experiences, and offboarding procedures. Verify privileged role activation, approval rules, expiration, administrative audit logs, denied access, and emergency recovery.
  4. Migrate and train: Move validated content and workflows in stages with rollback criteria, owner approval, training and published support procedures. Configure Azure Bastion only if private Azure VM administration is required by the selected infrastructure.
  5. Operate and review: Monitor adoption and sign-in events, review external access, test backups and recovery arrangements, and refine governance.
Cost and licensing caveat: This recommendation intentionally does not claim a current Microsoft 365 or Azure price. The final business-software recommendation should cite a chosen license configuration and dated vendor prices, including endpoint-management, Entra PIM, and security features where required. Dedicated PAWs and any optional Bastion deployment must be included in total cost of ownership.
