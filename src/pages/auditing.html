OUR APPROACH

IT Auditing

Our IT auditing service focuses on understanding how technology processes and controls support business objectives. The goal is to document evidence, identify meaningful gaps, and help management prioritize corrective action; engagements focus on practical findings and management-ready recommendations.

01

Process and control review

Define assessment scope and review access management, change procedures, backup practices, incident handling, and relevant system operations against documented expectations.

02

Documentation quality

Examine policies, architecture diagrams, inventories, approvals, and operating procedures for completeness and consistency. Record missing evidence without assuming that an undocumented control is functioning.

03

Risk observations

Describe observed control weaknesses, affected assets, likelihood and business impact assumptions, and compensating controls. Separate supported findings from recommendations.

04

Remediation planning

Assign corrective-action owners, prioritize work according to risk, propose verification methods, and establish dates for management to review closure evidence.
