About PCP

Founded in Las Vegas in 2015 by Joanne Schmidt and Alexis Green, Project Consultants Plus brings more than 30 years of combined software project-management experience to organizations navigating technology change. Our consulting model connects clients with experienced project leaders for engagements across the United States and internationally.

01

Experience-led delivery

PCP brings together the founders’ combined project-management experience and a network of specialized professionals to support real business and technology challenges.

02

Distributed consulting model

Consultants work across locations and client environments, making secure communication, dependable coordination, and centralized information essential.

03

Accountability to clients

Our consultants bring PMI-certified project management expertise to client engagements, backed by a 100% service guarantee.
