OUR APPROACH

Usability Testing

A system can meet its technical specifications and still be difficult to use. PCP's proposed usability practice examines real user tasks, information clarity, and accessibility so implementation teams can identify barriers before broad rollout.

01

User journeys

Identify representative user roles and map the steps they take to complete important tasks. Distinguish frequent workflows from high-risk or exceptional scenarios.

02

Task-based assessments

Observe whether users can find information, complete activities, and recover from mistakes. Capture task completion, friction points, and qualitative feedback rather than relying only on opinions.

03

Accessibility considerations

Review navigation, keyboard interaction, readable labels, contrast, and feedback messages. Document barriers that could prevent staff or customers from using the service effectively.

04

Findings and recommendations

Prioritize issues by business impact and user difficulty, propose corrective changes, and retest revised workflows before acceptance or deployment.
